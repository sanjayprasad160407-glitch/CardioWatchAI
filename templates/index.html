PROJECT WORKFLOW

How CardioWatch AI works

ECG→Filter→ML Model→Saliency→Alert

The software prototype accepts ECG CSV data, applies signal preprocessing, classifies a selected window with either the real trained CNN or the local demo ML model, records the event, and presents the result in the dashboard.

SAFETY

Important limitation

This is an educational/research prototype. Predictions, confidence values, alerts, and chatbot responses are not medical diagnoses, clinical validation, or emergency response. Do not use this software to make treatment decisions.
